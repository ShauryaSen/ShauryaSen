# Shaurya Sen

I study CS at Cornell and do reinforcement learning research with Prof. Sarah Dean. I work on simulating and training robots.

**Research**
- **Balloon navigation** with [Prof. Sarah Dean's group](https://github.com/sdean-group): a conditional diffusion model that generates realistic, effectively unbounded wind for training balloon-navigation RL agents ([balloon-env-dev](https://github.com/sdean-group/balloon-env-dev)), and controllers that plan under forecast error ([balloon-sim](https://github.com/ShauryaSen/balloon-sim)).
- **Legged locomotion** with [Cornell Physical Intelligence](https://github.com/Cornell-Physical-Intelligence): teaching our hexapod to walk in Isaac Lab ([hexapod-locomotion](https://github.com/ShauryaSen/hexapod-locomotion)) and an online AMP learner ([hexapod-cupi](https://github.com/Cornell-Physical-Intelligence/hexapod-cupi)).

**Projects**
- **Drone racing**: PPO in Flightmare, an IROS 2021 replication ([flightmare-vm](https://github.com/ShauryaSen/flightmare-vm)).
- **Marine search and rescue** at the West Point Robotics Research Center: YOLO on unmanned boats to find people overboard ([SNR-yolo-moos-integration](https://github.com/dejaa1/SNR-yolo-moos-integration), [report](https://shauryasen.github.io/papers/maritime-sar-report.pdf)).

Previously: two summers as a software engineer at Bloomberg.
